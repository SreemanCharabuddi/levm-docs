## Trading

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the maximum leverage?">
    5x on markets that pass our quality checks. Lower tiers (2x, 3x) are available on markets with less liquidity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I short a market?">
    Yes. Open a long NO position to effectively short the YES outcome.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I close my position before the event ends?">
    Yes, anytime. Manual close is available 24/7 until the pre-event close window begins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the pre-event close?">
    LevM automatically closes all leveraged positions 30 minutes before the underlying event begins. This is built into the platform and cannot be disabled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't LevM hold positions through resolution?">
    Prediction markets resolve to 0 or 100 cents instantly. Holding leveraged positions through that moment creates risks that no leverage platform has been able to manage safely. LevM avoids this entirely by closing pre-event.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Liquidation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is liquidation?">
    If your position's unrealized loss approaches your collateral, LevM closes the position automatically. This prevents you from losing more than you put in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I owe more than my collateral?">
    No. Your maximum loss is your collateral plus the liquidation penalty (5% of remaining collateral).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is the liquidation price calculated?">
    Based on your leverage, entry price, and maintenance margin. The exact price is shown when you open your position.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Platform safety

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is LevM safe?">
    LevM is currently a custodial platform with on-chain deposits and withdrawals. The team is committed to migrating to non-custodial smart contracts after the initial launch period. Users should only deposit what they're comfortable with the platform holding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know LevM is solvent?">
    Check the Solvency Dashboard. It shows the platform's custody balance, total user balances, and insurance fund in real time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if LevM has technical issues?">
    The platform may pause new position opens during outages while keeping existing positions and closes available. Critical issues are communicated through Discord and X.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
