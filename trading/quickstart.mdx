---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Open your first leveraged position in five steps."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Click **Connect Wallet** at the top right. LevM supports MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, and WalletConnect. Make sure you're on the Polygon network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC">
    Go to the Deposit page. You'll see LevM's custody address and a QR code. Send USDC on Polygon from your wallet to that address. Your balance updates within 60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a market">
    On the Trade page, browse the markets eligible for leverage. Each market shows the current YES probability, available leverage range, and recent volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a position">
    Choose YES or NO. Set your leverage (2x to 5x). Enter the amount of USDC to commit as collateral. LevM shows you:

    - Total position size
    - Your liquidation price
    - Estimated fees

    Click **Open Position** and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch and manage">
    Your position appears in the Positions page with live unrealized profit and loss. Close manually at any time by clicking **Close**. The platform will automatically close all positions 30 minutes before the event begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>
