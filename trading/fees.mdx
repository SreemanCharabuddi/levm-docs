title: "Fees"
description: "What LevM charges, where it charges, and a worked total-cost example."
---

LevM charges fees in a few places. All fees are disclosed before you open a position.

## Open spread

A small spread of 0.5 cents per contract, charged when you open. This is approximately 0.08% of position size on a market trading at 60 cents.

## Close spread

A matching 0.5 cents per contract, charged when you close. Same approximate cost as the open spread.

## Open fee

A 0.1% fee on the position size, charged at open. On a $1,000 position, that's $1.

## Funding rate

A small hourly fee accrued while your position is open. The rate ranges from 0.03% to 0.15% per hour depending on market conditions. Funding is charged from your available USDC balance.

## Liquidation penalty

If your position is liquidated, 5% of the remaining collateral is taken as a penalty. This goes to LevM's insurance fund, which protects the platform's solvency for all users.

## No other fees

LevM does not charge:

- Deposit fees
- Withdrawal fees (you pay network gas only)
- Close fees beyond the close spread
- Inactivity fees
- Account fees

## Total cost example

Open a $1,000 position, hold for 8 hours, close manually:

| Item                          | Cost      |
| ----------------------------- | --------- |
| Open spread                   | ~$0.80    |
| Open fee                      | $1.00     |
| Funding (8 hrs at 0.08%/hr)   | $6.40     |
| Close spread                  | ~$0.80    |
| **Total**                     | **~$9.00** |

<Tip>
  Less than 1% of position size for a typical trade.
</Tip>
